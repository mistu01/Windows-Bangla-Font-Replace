========================================================================
                      PUT YOUR CUSTOM BENGALI FONT HERE
========================================================================

Drop your Bengali font (.ttf or .otf) right here in this folder!

Examples:
- If you have 1 font:
    Kalpurush.ttf (or SolaimanLipi.ttf, Siyamrupali.ttf, etc.)

- If you have Regular and Bold:
    MyFont-Regular.ttf
    MyFont-Bold.ttf

Once your font file is placed here, run "replace_bangla_font.bat" from the main folder!
